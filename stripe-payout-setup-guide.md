# Shopify Payments & Stripe Setup Guide

This guide outlines the steps required to configure Shopify Payments (powered by Stripe) to receive payouts for The Barnacle store.

## Prerequisites

Before beginning the setup process, ensure you have the following information readily available:
- Employer Identification Number (EIN) or Social Security Number (SSN)
- Business address and contact information
- Bank account routing number and account number for payouts
- Personal identification details for the primary business owner

## Step-by-Step Configuration

### 1. Access Payment Settings
Log in to your Shopify Admin dashboard. Navigate to the bottom left corner and click on **Settings**, then select **Payments** from the left-hand menu.

### 2. Activate Shopify Payments
In the Payments section, locate the "Shopify Payments" block. Click the **Activate Shopify Payments** button. This will initiate the onboarding process for the integrated Stripe payment gateway.

### 3. Enter Business Details
You will be prompted to provide comprehensive business information. Select your business type (e.g., LLC, Sole Proprietorship) and enter your EIN or SSN as required. Provide the official business address and a brief description of the products sold (e.g., "Beverage holders and accessories").

### 4. Provide Personal Information
Shopify requires details about the primary account owner for identity verification purposes. Enter your full legal name, date of birth, and home address. This information is securely processed to comply with financial regulations.

### 5. Configure Payout Details
Enter the routing number and account number of the business bank account where you wish to receive funds. Ensure these numbers are accurate to prevent payout delays. You can also configure your payout schedule (e.g., daily, weekly, or monthly) based on your cash flow preferences.

### 6. Set Up Customer Billing Statement
Define how charges will appear on your customers' bank or credit card statements. It is recommended to use a clear identifier, such as "THE BARNACLE CO", to minimize confusion and potential chargebacks. Include a customer service phone number if applicable.

### 7. Complete Setup and Verification
Review all entered information for accuracy. Click **Complete account setup** to submit your application. Shopify and Stripe will review your details. In some cases, you may be asked to upload additional documentation, such as a government-issued ID or a business license, to finalize the verification process.

## Managing Payouts

Once approved, you can monitor your payouts directly from the Shopify Admin. Navigate to **Settings > Payments** and click **View payouts** in the Shopify Payments section. Here, you can track upcoming deposits, view historical payout data, and reconcile transactions with your bank statements.
