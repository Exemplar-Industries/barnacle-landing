CANVA TEMPLATE ANALYSIS — "8.5x11 BARNACLE TEMPLATE NEWEST"
============================================================

TEMPLATE STRUCTURE (45 pages total):
- Page 1: Full template with gray background — shows the complete ring disc design
- Page 2: White background version — shows the ring outline/guide circle and Barnacle logo fish
- Page 3: Repeating "THE BARNACLE.co" text arcs (mirrored/flipped) — these are the background watermark text layers
- Page 4: "SAMPLE NAME / XXX, XX" version — shows where customer name and location text go
- Page 5: "POWER MOVE / CAPE MAY, NJ" — a real completed example with boat/marlin icons

KEY DESIGN STRUCTURE:
- Outer ring disc (gray): Large circle, fills most of the 8.5x11 page
- Red ring: Thin red circle border separating the gray outer area from the black inner ring
- Black ring: The main customizable ring band (annular zone between red circle and white center)
- White center circle: The hole/cutout where the silicone cup sits through
- Center dot: Small registration/center mark

TEXT PLACEMENT:
- TOP ARC: "THE BARNACLE.co" in handwritten/graffiti font, curves along top of outer gray ring
- BOTTOM ARC: Customer's custom text (e.g. "SAMPLE NAME" / "POWER MOVE") in block/collegiate font
- BOTTOM SECOND LINE: Location/subtitle text (e.g. "XXX, XX" / "CAPE MAY, NJ") in same collegiate font

LAYER STRUCTURE (from page 3 — the text layer page):
- Multiple mirrored/rotated versions of "THE BARNACLE.co" text arcs
- These form the repeating background watermark pattern on the gray ring area

PROPORTIONS (estimated from 8.5x11 canvas):
- Canvas: 8.5" x 11" = 816 x 1056px at 96dpi, or 2550 x 3300px at 300dpi
- Outer disc diameter: ~7" (fills most of the width)
- Red ring outer diameter: ~4.8"
- Black ring inner diameter (white center): ~3.2"
- The printable/customizable zone is the GRAY OUTER RING (between outer edge and red ring)
- The BLACK RING is also customizable (image/pattern goes here)
- The WHITE CENTER is the cutout — nothing should be designed here (it's the hole)

WHAT CUSTOMER CUSTOMIZES:
1. Background pattern/image on the BLACK RING zone
2. Custom text on the BOTTOM ARC of the GRAY OUTER RING
3. Font choice for the bottom arc text

WHAT IS FIXED (always printed):
1. "THE BARNACLE.co" top arc text
2. Red ring border
3. The repeating watermark text in the gray area background

BACKEND AUTOMATION OPPORTUNITY:
- Customer submits: custom text, font, optional image/pattern
- System needs to: populate the Canva template layers with this data
- Canva API (or image compositing) can update: the bottom text layer, the black ring image layer
- Output: print-ready PDF at 300dpi matching the template exactly

KEY INSIGHT FROM PAGE 7 (SUNSET VERSION):
The template has TWO modes:
1. GRAY BACKGROUND mode: gray disc + black ring + customer image fills just the ring zone
2. PHOTO BACKGROUND mode: customer photo fills the ENTIRE disc circle, text overlays on top

The live preview configurator should match the PHOTO BACKGROUND mode since that's what customers upload.
When a photo is uploaded, it should fill the entire disc (clipped to circle), with:
- "THE BARNACLE.co" arc text at top (fixed, always shown)
- Customer name arc text at bottom
- White center hole punched out

PROPORTIONS (refined from page 7 sunset version):
- The disc is a perfect circle
- Center hole = approximately 30% of disc diameter
- The "THE BARNACLE.co" text arc radius = approximately 85% of disc radius
- The customer name text arc radius = approximately 85% of disc radius (bottom)
- Text sits in the outer ring area, between the center hole and the disc edge
